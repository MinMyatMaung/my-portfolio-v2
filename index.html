About Me

I am a Computer Science student with a strong interest in full-stack development and creating applications that solve real-world problems. I enjoy working on projects that challenge me to think critically and apply what I’ve learned in meaningful ways.


Through coursework, hackathons, and personal projects, I have gained experience working with technologies such as JavaScript, React, Node.js, and Python. I enjoy developing both frontend interfaces and backend logic, and I like understanding how different parts of an application work together. I am especially interested in full-stack development because it allows me to see a project from concept to completion.


I believe that learning never stops in technology, and that is one of the reasons why I love this field. I am actively seeking opportunities to grow as a developer, collaborate with others, and apply my skills in practical settings. My goal is to continue improving as a programmer while contributing to projects that have a positive impact and provide meaningful user experiences.
